Lightning Lane Pass Guide (For Bowie - Age 6)

Top-priority selections optimized for age 6 to minimize standby waiting. Pre-booking opens 7 days before check-in for Disney Resort guests!

Hollywood Studios (Sun Nov 1) 🎬

  • Slinky Dog Dash: #1 Priority pre-selection! Very high standby lines. Tier 1 Choice
  • Mickey & Minnie’s Runaway Railway: High charm dark ride, fun for the whole family. Tier 2 Choice
  • Toy Story Mania!: Low-stress interactive 3D carnival game ride with no height minimum. Tier 2 Choice
  • Alien Swirling Saucers: Fun spinning ride in Toy Story Land; great quick follow-up pass. Tier 2 Choice

EPCOT (Mon Nov 2 & Tue Nov 3) 🌍

  • Remy’s Ratatouille Adventure: Top Tier 1 pick! Right near your International Gateway entrance. Tier 1 Choice
  • Frozen Ever After: Can be selected as Tier 1 instead of Remy if Frozen is a favorite. Tier 1 Alt
  • Soarin’ Around the World: Smooth, gentle flying simulator over global landmarks. Tier 2 Choice
  • Journey Into Imagination With Figment: Zero wait dark ride; perfect mid-day low-sensory pick. Tier 2 Choice

Magic Kingdom (Wed Nov 4) 🏰

  • Peter Pan’s Flight: High priority dark ride with long standby queues. Tier 1 Choice
  • Big Thunder Mountain Railroad: Great "first coaster" thrills for 6-year-olds (40" height minimum). Tier 1 Alt
  • Buzz Lightyear’s Space Ranger Spin: Fun laser-shooting ride in Tomorrowland. Tier 2 Choice
  • The Many Adventures of Winnie the Pooh: Gentle dark ride that skips afternoon waits. Tier 2 Choice
  • Seven Dwarfs Mine Train: Purchased separately as a Single Pass; ideal smooth family coaster. Single Pass

Nighttime Spectaculars 🎆

  • Fantasmic! (Mon Nov 2): Live nighttime show at Hollywood Studios.
  • Luminous The Symphony of Us (Tue Nov 3): Fireworks at EPCOT World Showcase.
  • Happily Ever After (Wed Nov 4): 8:00 PM Magic Kingdom Castle Fireworks.

Kid-Friendly Lightning Lane Pass Guide (Ages ~6–7)

Top-priority selections optimized for kids around age 6 to save the maximum amount of line waiting for high-demand, age-appropriate attractions. As Disney Resort guests, pre-booking opens 7 days before check-in!

Hollywood Studios 🎬

  • Slinky Dog Dash: #1 Priority pre-selection! Very high standby lines. (38" height requirement) Tier 1 Choice
  • Mickey & Minnie’s Runaway Railway: High charm dark ride, fun for the whole family. Tier 2 Choice
  • Toy Story Mania!: Low-stress interactive 3D carnival game ride with no height minimum. Tier 2 Choice
  • Alien Swirling Saucers: Fun spinning ride in Toy Story Land; great quick follow-up pass during the day. Tier 2 Choice

EPCOT 🌍

  • Remy’s Ratatouille Adventure: Top Tier 1 pick! Right near your International Gateway entrance. Tier 1 Choice
  • Frozen Ever After: Can be selected as Tier 1 instead of Remy if Frozen is a higher favorite. Tier 1 Alt
  • Soarin’ Around the World: Smooth, gentle flying simulator over global landmarks (40" height requirement). Tier 2 Choice
  • Journey Into Imagination With Figment: Zero wait dark ride; perfect mid-day low-sensory pick. Tier 2 Choice

Magic Kingdom 🏰

  • Peter Pan’s Flight: Infamously long standby queues; ideal Tier 1 pre-booking. Tier 1 Choice
  • Big Thunder Mountain Railroad: Great "first coaster" thrills for 6-year-olds (40" height requirement). Tier 1 Alt
  • Buzz Lightyear’s Space Ranger Spin: Fun laser-shooting ride in Tomorrowland. Tier 2 Choice
  • The Many Adventures of Winnie the Pooh: Gentle dark ride that skips 45+ min afternoon waits. Tier 2 Choice
  • Seven Dwarfs Mine Train: Purchased separately as a Single Pass; ideal smooth coaster for this age. Single Pass

Animal Kingdom (Mon Backup) 🦁

  • Na’vi River Journey: Beautiful, smooth boat ride in Pandora (no height limit). Tier 1 Choice
  • Kilimanjaro Safaris: Best scheduled for morning hours when animals are most active. Tier 2 Choice
  • Festival of the Lion King: World-class indoor musical show; Lightning Lane guarantees priority seating. Tier 2 Choice

Vegetarian & Plant-Based Food Guide

Disney World offers official plant-based items (made with zero animal meat, dairy, eggs, or honey) marked with a green leaf logo 🌱, as well as easy vegetarian adjustments across table service and quick-service spots.

Beaches & Cream Plant-Based Friendly

  • Impossible Burger: Plant-based patty on an allergy-friendly bun with seasonal relish & plant mayo. Vegan
  • Loaded Plant-Based Tots: Tots topped with plant-based chili, dairy-free cheese, sour cream, and green onions. Vegan
  • Beaches & Cream Grilled Cheese: Classic melted cheese served with creamy tomato soup. Vegetarian
  • Cookie Fries & Non-Dairy Ice Cream: Crispy cookie fries with dips, plus non-dairy coconut/soy ice cream scoops & sundaes. Vegan

Blizzard Beach (Lottawatta Lodge & Kiosks) Water Park Picks

  • Impossible® Burger (Lottawatta Lodge): Served with plant-based chipotle mayonnaise, lettuce, and tomato with house-made chips. Vegan
  • Winter Harvest Salad (Lottawatta Lodge): Kale & lettuce blend, grape tomatoes, red radishes, rainbow carrots, raisins, quinoa, dates, and pear vinaigrette. Vegan
  • Hummus Snack Tray (Lottawatta Lodge & Avalunch): Red pepper hummus paired with fresh carrots, celery, pretzels, and mini pita bread. Vegan
  • Pineapple Dole Whip (Frostbite Freddie's): Refreshing, dairy-free frozen soft-serve perfect for water park days. Vegan
  • Mini Donuts (Joffrey’s Stand): Hot mini donuts (request plain cinnamon sugar or raspberry dip; chocolate/white chocolate dips contain dairy). Vegetarian

Animal Kingdom (Mon Backup Options) Park Picks

  • Satu'li Canteen (Pandora): Chili-Spiced Tofu Bowl with potato hash or grain blend and boba topping—one of the best quick service meals in Disney! Vegan
  • Flame Tree Barbecue: Impossible™ Burrito Bowl topped with cilantro rice, black beans, and salsa. Vegan
  • Tamu Tamu Refreshments: Pineapple Dole Whip Cups and Dole Whip Floats. Vegan

Roundup Rodeo BBQ Dedicated Veg Skillet

  • Trixie’s Plant-Based Trio (Platter): Full BBQ platter featuring Combat Crisp Plant-Based Sausage, Slow-smoked Cauliflower, and Rip-Roarin' Jackfruit. Vegan
  • Rootin’ Tootin’ Tomato Salad & Veggie Slaw: Fresh cucumbers, tomatoes, and "The Claw!" crunchy veggie slaw. Vegan
  • Buckin' Baked Beans & Corn on Cob: Vegetarian baked beans and grilled street corn. Vegetarian
  • Slinky Doooooooooog Mac & Cheese: Creamy classic baked mac & cheese. Vegetarian
  • Gruff’s Peach-Strawberry Pie: Plant-based dessert served in a jar. Vegan

Sci-Fi Dine-In Theater Fan Favorite

  • Flying Saucer Impossible Burger: Plant-based burger topped with grilled portobello mushroom steak, cashew mozzarella, garlic-tomato aioli, and pesto. Vegan
  • Spinach & Artichoke Dip: Creamy cashew-based dip served with warm tortilla chips. Vegan
  • Crispy Onion Rings: Ask for the plant-based batter version. Vegan
  • Out-of-this-World Cookie Milkshake: Plant-based milkshake made with non-dairy milk, plant ice cream, and topped with an Oreo-style cookie! Vegan

The Edison (Disney Springs) Customizable

  • The Beyond Burger: Plant-based burger patty served with lettuce, tomato, pickles, and fries. Vegetarian
  • Beyond Sausage & Pepper Pasta: Hearty pasta dish with plant-based sausage and savory marinara sauce. Vegetarian

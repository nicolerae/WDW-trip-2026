Saturday 10/31 Arrival Strategies

Magic Kingdom closes early for the sold-out Halloween Party. Choose your arrival route based on flight timing:

Animal Kingdom Half-Day

Tap in by 12:30 PM | Park closes early

  • Lowest-stress park option if flight schedule experiences minor delays.
  • Pandora, Kilimanjaro Safaris, and Lion King show make a complete afternoon.
  • Earliest park closure gives an early bedtime before rope-drop day.
Action Required: Park ticket for 10/31 + Satu'li Canteen Mobile Order.

Water Park Afternoon

Arrive by 11:00 AM for Blizzard Beach

  • Immediate splash/swim payoff right after traveling.
  • No long walking marathon on day one.
  • Frees Monday 11/02 to become a second theme-park day or full rest day.
Action Required: Water park add-on + swimsuits packed in carry-on.

Disney Springs + Resort Pool

Anytime Arrival — No Ticket Required

  • Works no matter when your flight lands or when hotel room is ready.
  • Explore World of Disney, LEGO Store, and Marketplace Carousel.
  • Low-pressure dinner reservation and early bedtime.
Action Required: Disney Springs dinner reservation.
